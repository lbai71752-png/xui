Our Planet Is Warming

The Earth is now about 1.1°C hotter than in pre-industrial times. Glaciers are melting, seas are rising, and extreme weather is escalating. The time to act is now.

Take Action

Why It Matters

Global warming is not a distant threat - its effects are already here.

Extreme Heat

Heatwaves are more frequent and intense, threatening health, crops and power grids.

Rising Seas

Melting ice sheets raise sea levels, putting coastal cities and island nations at risk.

Species Loss

Shifting climates destroy habitats - around 1 million species face extinction.

What You Can Do

Small everyday choices add up. Every kilowatt-hour saved cuts about 0.8 kg of CO2.

Travel Green

Walk, cycle or take public transport whenever you can.

Save Energy

Switch off lights, unplug devices, choose efficient appliances.

Reduce & Recycle

Cut single-use plastics and sort your waste properly.

Spread the Word

Share what you know and inspire others to join in.

Be a Planet Cooler

Together, our everyday actions can change the climate story. Start with one promise today.
